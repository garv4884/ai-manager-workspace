# Privacy Policy: AI Manager Workspace

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

AI Manager Workspace ("the app") is a mixed-reality app for Meta Quest that lets you manage a team of AI agents.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers operated by the developer. We never receive your data.

## What is stored on your headset
Settings, your agent team, desk positions, task history and (optionally) API keys you enter are stored **locally on your device**. Clearing the app's data in Quest settings, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

## Hand tracking and sensors
The app uses hand tracking, head pose and (optionally) passthrough to let you interact. This data is processed **on the device by the Meta system**. The app does not record, store or transmit camera images or tracking data.

## Optional third-party AI services
The app works fully offline in demo mode. **Only if you choose to**, you can enter your own API key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or another OpenAI-compatible service. In that case the text of tasks and agent conversations is sent directly from your headset to that provider to generate responses, under that provider's terms and privacy policy. We do not see or store this traffic. Do not enter confidential information you would not want to send to that provider.

## Optional PC bridge
If you run the optional Agent Bridge on your own computer, the app connects to it over your local network or USB. Requests you approve or answer are exchanged between the app and your bridge. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information.
